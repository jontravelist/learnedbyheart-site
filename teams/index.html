Gifts for teams

Most end-of-year gifts are forgotten by February. A novel written for one person isn't. Each person on your team, or each client, gets an original novel in the style of the books they love, built around something worth understanding.

What it can teach

  • Money: pensions, investing, how interest really works. A gentle way into financial wellbeing that people actually finish.
  • Online safety: how phishing and scams work, told as a thriller. Read the free first chapter of Click Here to Verify.
  • AI and technology: what these tools do and where they go wrong. See the technology shelf.
  • Or your own field: tell us what you'd like your people to understand.

How it works

  1. Email us how many books, your deadline, and the subject or subjects.
  2. We send one short form link for each person. They (or you) answer a few questions about the books they love. It takes about ten minutes.
  3. Chapter one arrives within 24 hours of each form. The whole book follows within three days, delivered privately to each reader on the date you choose.

Ten books or more: we quote a team price and send one invoice. For two or three, the three-novel gift bundle is the simplest way.

Ask about team books

Or write to hello@learnedbyheart.com. We answer within a day.

Good to know

  • Each reader's answers and book are private. We don't share them with you or anyone else unless the reader chooses to.
  • We design each story from the reader's answers and write it with the help of AI tools, then check every chapter for story, clarity and facts.
  • It's a story that teaches, not financial, legal or security advice.
  • Everyone in the story gets a made-up name, and places stay at town or city level.
