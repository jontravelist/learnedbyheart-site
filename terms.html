What you get

A single novel ($12.99)

  • An illustrated novel of three to four hours' reading, written for you or for the person you name (the recipient). It teaches a subject you choose through the plot, with short questions along the way.
  • A preview of chapter one before the rest is written, and one round of changes.
  • Chapter one read aloud.
  • Delivery as a private web link.

The audiobook (optional, $6.99, added when you pay)

  • Every chapter of your novel read aloud by a computer-generated voice, delivered with the novel.

Founding membership ($19 a month)

  • One novel a month written for you, either new or remixed from a library title, with the same preview, one round of changes and the audiobook included.
  • Every library book while you're a member.
  • The founding price for as long as your membership continues without a break.

  • Your membership renews automatically each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, and it ends at the end of the month you've paid for. Books already delivered stay yours.

Each novel comes as a private web link and as an ebook file (EPUB) you can download and keep. The ebook has the same words and pictures; its questions are printed, with the answers at the back. The audiobook is in the web version only. The ebook is for your own use: please don't share or resell it. There is no printed copy yet.

Your order and the order limit

We take a limited number of orders (currently 10 single novels before Christmas, and 20 founding members). Your order is accepted when we email to confirm it. If the limit has been reached and you have already paid, we refund you in full within 14 days.

Payments are taken by our payment provider, Stripe, which also works out and collects any sales tax. Prices are in US dollars and include tax; checkout can show your own currency.

Delivery

We send chapter one within 24 hours of receiving your answers, and the whole book within three days of your approving it. For Christmas, order by 16 December: we aim to email the link by 23 December, or schedule it for Christmas morning if you ask. Please answer the questions promptly. If they arrive late, we'll tell you if we can't meet the date.

You can ask for one round of reasonable changes to chapter one, such as names, tone, details or things to avoid. Further changes are at our discretion.
