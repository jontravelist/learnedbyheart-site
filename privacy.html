Learned by Heart Terms of sale

Privacy note

Last updated 17 November 2026

This note explains how we use personal information when you order a story. It covers you (the buyer) and the person the story is for (the recipient).

Who we are

Learned by Heart is run by Listed Creative, a company registered in Singapore. We're responsible for your information under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act and, where they apply to you, the UK and EU data protection laws. Contact us at hello@learnedbyheart.com.

What we collect

About you, the buyer

  • your name and email address;
  • your order details, delivery date and any messages you send us;
  • confirmation that you paid. Card details go straight to our payment provider; we never see or store them.

About the recipient, from your answers

  • first name (used only in the dedication and our emails, never in the story itself);
  • tastes in books, films and themes;
  • things to avoid;
  • a few light personal facts to weave into the story.

Please don't include anything about health, sexuality, religion or beliefs, politics, ethnicity, trade union membership, genetics, biometrics or criminal records. If you send it by mistake, we delete it.

For the recipient, we keep the details light, use them for that one story only, keep the book at a private link, and delete your answers soon after delivery. People generally expect friends and family to share small details like these for a gift.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising, and we don't make automated decisions about you. A person reviews every story.

Who we share it with

Each provider gets only what it needs.

  • Stripe takes payment and handles sales tax. It may also use your details for its own purposes, such as fraud checks, under its own privacy policy.
  • Tally collects your answers to the order questions.
  • AI providers (such as Anthropic and Google) help write the story, paint the pictures and read the audio. We send the pictures only what they need, not personal details, and we use settings that stop providers training their models on your answers where they offer them.
  • Our hosting providers (GitHub for this website, Netlify for finished books) serve the pages.
  • Our email provider sends you messages and your link.

We'll also share information if the law requires it.

The private link

Your book lives at an unlisted web link. It isn't listed or searchable, but anyone with the link can open it, so please share it only with people you trust. The ebook you download is a file on your own device; we can't see or delete copies once downloaded.

International transfers

Some providers store or process data outside Singapore, the UK or the EU, for example in the USA. When that happens, we rely on legal safeguards such as approved contract terms. Ask us if you'd like the details.

How long we keep it

  • Your answers to the questions: deleted 60 days after delivery. If you ask us to keep them for changes or a follow-on story, we keep them until you tell us to delete them, and for no more than 12 months.
  • The book: stays online until you ask us to take it down.
  • Library editions: six months after delivery, unless you opted out, we may publish a library edition with the dedication removed and anything identifying changed. It holds no personal information about you or the recipient, and you can ask us to remove it.
  • Order and payment records: kept for as long as tax law requires, usually up to seven years.
  • Emails with you: kept for up to two years after your order, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, including where we rely on legitimate interests;
  • give it to you in a usable format, where that applies.

Email hello@learnedbyheart.com. We reply within one month, and it's usually free.

The recipient's rights

The recipient has the same rights over their own information. They can contact us to see what we hold, correct it, or ask us to delete it and take the book down. Because the story is usually a surprise, we don't contact recipients before it's delivered. If the recipient is a child, a parent or guardian can act for them.

Complaints

Please contact us first so we can try to put things right. You can also complain to Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (pdpc.gov.sg), or to the data protection regulator where you live, such as the ICO in the UK (ico.org.uk).

Changes

We may update this note. We'll post the new version here with the date.
