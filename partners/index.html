Learned by Heart Terms of sale

Become a partner

Learned by Heart writes novels for one reader at a time: a real novel in the style they love, that quietly teaches the thing they've always meant to learn. If you write about books, run a newsletter, or share what you read, you can earn from every order your readers make.

What you get

  • 20% of every order that comes through your link: $2.60 on a $12.99 novel, $4.00 with the audiobook.
  • Your link works on any page of the site, including the free first chapters of all 50 library novels, and we remember it for 30 days after someone clicks.
  • Paid monthly by PayPal once you're owed $20 or more.

Join as a partner

It takes two minutes. We email your link straight away.

Partner terms

  1. Commission. 20% of what the buyer paid for a novel or audiobook ordered through your link, excluding tax. Memberships and gift bundles bought directly through Stripe are not included.
  2. When it's paid. Commission becomes payable once the whole book has been delivered and the 30-day refund window has passed. Refunded or cancelled orders earn nothing. We pay by PayPal each month once you're owed $20 or more.
  3. Credit. An order is credited to the last partner link the buyer clicked in the 30 days before ordering. Your own orders don't count.
  4. Be clear. Say plainly when you share a partner link, for example "affiliate link" or #ad, as the law where you live requires. Don't promise things we don't offer, and don't bid on "Learned by Heart" in search ads.
  5. No spam. Don't send your link to people who haven't asked to hear from you.
  6. Changes and ending. We may change these terms with 30 days' notice by email, and either of us can end the partnership at any time. Commission you've already earned is still paid.
  7. Your details. We keep your name, email, website and PayPal address only to run the partnership and pay you. See our privacy note.
