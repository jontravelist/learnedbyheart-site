Novels that teach money

Thrillers, romances, comedies and sagas where the plot runs on how money really works. Each one teaches one subject through a story you would read anyway, a chapter at a time. Subjects here: accounting, bubbles and crashes, buying a home, inflation and how economies work, money across a lifetime, pensions and retirement saving, running a small business, scams and fraud.
